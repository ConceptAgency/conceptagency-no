Making AI part of your operations, one step at a time.

Concept Agency Consulting works with owner-led companies that have grown faster than their systems. We connect the tools you already run and put AI on top, so the operation gets smarter as it scales. Erik Spade leads the work, as a single sprint or as your fractional COO.

01 — The situation

Growth outruns the systems.

What we see in most companies with 5 to 50 people

The order platform, the accounting system and the CRM each hold part of the truth, and spreadsheets fill the gaps. The owner is the integration layer. Some have tried AI; few have made it part of how the work gets done.

A full-time COO is early and expensive. A systems vendor installs what it is asked to install. What is missing is someone senior who works it out with you: what to connect, in what order, and where AI earns its place.

02 — The approach

Together we work out what to build, and in what order.

Every engagement starts with you and your team. We map how the work flows today and agree where the time and money go. What we add is the experience to know which integrations to build, in what sequence, and how to put AI on top so the operation gets smarter as it scales.

05 — Client case: monthly retainer

Fractional COO for a Nordic multi-brand distributor

Ongoing engagement since 2025

The client is a Nordic sales and distribution partner for more than 15 international brands, with over 400 customers across the Nordics and a small team selling to chains, independent retailers and direct to consumers. Erik is its fractional COO a fixed number of days each month, alongside the owners. The mandate has two parts: tidy up operations, and build AI into the workflows — not as a pilot, but as the way the team actually works.
