For Retail

A short list. One standard: worth shelf space.

Every brand below was chosen for the same reason — it gives your store a point of view. From Parisian eyewear to a Japanese design icon and one-off Berlin glass, this is a portfolio built for concept stores, museum shops, interiors retailers and design-led boutiques across the Nordics.

01 — Eyewear

Izipizi

Paris, France

Eyewear for every chapter of life — sunglasses, reading and screen glasses in a colour palette that sells itself at the counter. Designed in Paris, loved worldwide, and priced for repeat purchase.

From newborn to grandparent, Izipizi covers the entire family — which makes it one of the rare brands that performs equally well in a concept store, a bookshop and a museum gift shop.

Right for Concept stores · museum shops · bookshops · lifestyle boutiques · garden & outdoor retail
02 — Gift & Interior

Hippie de Luxe

Sweden — formerly Ohlsson & Lohaven

The Nordics' leading gift article house, representing 33 brands under one roof. Interiors and gift articles with personality — curated collections that let a single buyer fill a whole department with one order.

For the retailer, the value is efficiency: one relationship, one delivery, dozens of brands — all pre-selected for Nordic taste.

Right for Gift & interior stores · department stores · garden centres · lifestyle retail
03 — Furniture

Nychair X

Japan — designed by Takeshi Nii, 1970

The Japanese folding lounge chair that earned a permanent place in design history. Made entirely in Japan since 1970 — foldable, repairable, and beautiful whether open or closed.

An anchor product: Nychair X gives a store a destination piece — the object customers come in to see, photograph and return to buy. Some things are bought once and kept for life.

Right for Design & furniture stores · museum shops · interiors retail · architecture bookshops
04 — Concept Art

Dana Wyse

Paris / Vancouver

Limited-edition concept art with wit and bite — collectible pieces that sit at the intersection of art object and conversation starter. Each work is a small provocation, beautifully packaged.

Dana Wyse gives museum shops and galleries something genuinely rare: art-world credibility at a giftable price point.

Right for Museum shops · galleries · concept stores · design bookshops
05 — Glass

Milena Kling

Berlin, Germany

Contemporary mouth-blown glass, signed and one of a kind. Each piece is shaped in a soft copper mould, in sand or in raw rock — processes that cannot be repeated exactly, so no two objects are alike.

Work held in the permanent collection of the Pinakothek der Moderne, and on the tables at Maaemo in Oslo. For a store it is the piece that proves the room has a point of view — art-world credibility, with a range that runs from gifting to collectible.

Right for Museum & gallery shops · design retail · interiors & architects · fine dining
