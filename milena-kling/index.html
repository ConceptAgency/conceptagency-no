01 —

Glass that is made once, and kept.

Studio Milena Kling has worked in mouth-blown glass since 2012. Each piece is shaped in a soft copper mould, in sand, or in raw rock — processes that cannot be repeated exactly, so no two objects are alike. Every piece of the Studio Collection is signed.

The studio works with a small number of traditional hot shops in Berlin, Sweden and Bohemia, and finishes, hand-packs and ships every piece from Berlin. Several collections are made wholly or partly from rescued and recycled glass — the Overflow coupes are made from the discarded by-product of standard glass production.

The work sits between design and art. It is bought by restaurants, by design retail, by interior architects and by collectors — and it belongs in museum and gallery shops that sell objects, not merchandise.
