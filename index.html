Nychair X
A Japanese design icon, since 1970

Designed by Takeshi Nii in 1970 and still made entirely in Japan, Nychair X is the folding lounge chair that earned a permanent place in design history — comfortable, repairable, and beautiful folded or open.

We are bringing Nychair X to the Nordic market — for design stores, museum shops and interiors retailers who understand that some objects are bought once and kept for life.
